WHAT IS SAFEDRIVE?

A VEHICLE
THAT CAN
CALL FOR HELP.

SafeDrive is an intelligent vehicle safety system designed to detect an accident, identify the vehicle's location and initiate an emergency response.

Instead of waiting for someone to manually report an incident, SafeDrive creates a connected path from the vehicle to the people responsible for responding.

DETECT LOCATE VERIFY ALERT RESPOND
